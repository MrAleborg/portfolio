# Portfolio

Alexandre Le Borgne's public portfolio: who he is, his resume, and how to reach him, in French and English.

## Language

**Page**:
One of the public views of the site: Home, Resume or Contact. Each exists once per Locale.
_Avoid_: Screen, view, route

**Locale**:
The language a Page is shown in, French or English. The Page's address names it.
_Avoid_: Language setting, lang

**Canonical domain**:
The single address search engines should index the site under: alexandreleborgne.fr. Every other host serving the site is a duplicate.
_Avoid_: Main site, prod URL

**Page summary**:
What search engines and link previews show for a Page: its title, description and image, plus who the site is about.
_Avoid_: SEO tags, meta, head
